STELLAR · WEB3

Product Passport

A Stellar Testnet application for verifiable product ownership, transferable warranty rights and authorized service history.

Engineering scope
Built a Soroban contract, a TypeScript SDK and a Next.js reference app with Freighter signing and QR-based verification.
Design decision
Ownership transfer and warranty policy execute atomically in the contract; the SDK and interface use the contract’s result.
